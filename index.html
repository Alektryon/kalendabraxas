How to read this calendar

Each year begins on December 25. Its 364 ordinary days form four seasons of 91 days, seven planetary periods of 52 days and thirteen months of 28 days. The last ordinary day is December 23.

December 24, the Day of Abraxas, stands outside all three cycles and the daily correspondence. Its year is the Abraxas year that is ending.

In a Gregorian leap year, June 24 is the Day of Hermes. February 29 remains an ordinary day; the count pauses only on June 24. Hermes also stands outside every cycle.

The daily correspondence advances one element, one planet and one arcanum per ordinary day, beginning with Water · Sun · Magus on December 25. It resets at the start of each year; neither special day advances it.

Year 1 AA begins on December 25, 336 CE. The year number equals the Gregorian year at the start of the Abraxas year minus 335. Earlier dates follow this arithmetic through year 0 and negative AA years.

Gregorian dates use the proleptic Gregorian calendar, for CE years 1–9999. Dates update in both directions immediately. On special days the month and day fields are blank; select a month or day to return to an ordinary date.
